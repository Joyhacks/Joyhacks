# OFFFREQ — brand notes

**Name:** OFFFREQ (pronounced "off-frequency")  
**Descriptor:** An independent listening room.  
**Promise:** Good music. No shortcuts.  
**Signature line:** Tune out. Find more.

## The idea

An antidote to impersonal recommendation feeds. OFFFREQ behaves like a small record shop with someone who actually loves the music behind the counter. Calm utility meets irreverent independent-print energy.

## Art direction

- Clear, confident, slightly unconventional editorial hierarchy.
- Heavy sans display lettering contrasted with an expressive italic serif.
- Thin rules, serial numbers, tiny monospaced labels and album-like compositions.
- Warm paper canvas anchored by a near-black navigation and player.
- Acid chartreuse marks the moments where listening begins.
- Original bold-colour record sleeves, each art-directed rather than generated from a repeated card template.
- Zero glass effects, iridescent gradients, decorative shadows on buttons, or stock dashboards.

## Voice

Write like someone with excellent taste who doesn't need to prove it. Human, specific and lightly playful. No "AI-powered", "revolutionary", "unlock", or generic SaaS promise language.

## Product character

**Do:** reward curiosity, make the track/artist relationship clear, keep playback available, make saving immediate.  
**Don't:** force sign-in to browse, overload with infinite carousels, pass fictional demo samples off as licensed releases.

## Wordmark and mark

Lowercase **offfreq.** in heavy sans with tight tracking and a lime full stop. The four-axis asterisk suggests a finding signal and can stand alone as a favicon. Logo is available in `public/offfreq-logo.svg`; the in-app version is rendered as CSS/HTML for crisp scaling.

## Suggested portfolio copy

**OFFFREQ — Curating the unexpected.**

An original editorial music discovery interface developed in Next.js. The experience takes cues from independent music publications and record sleeve design rather than conventional streaming dashboards. It features responsive discovery, working search and mood filters, playable demo queues, and a saved-music library without mandatory sign-in. A supplied starter project was heavily refactored and given new design systems, UI components and artwork.

**Role:** Brand direction, UI/UX, frontend redevelopment, artwork system, interaction design.  
**Project type:** Independent concept / adapted frontend, not commissioned by Spotify or the fictional artists.
